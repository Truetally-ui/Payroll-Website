The other half of the problem

Paying your staff correctly is only useful if you get paid too.

NDIS providers who've fixed their payroll often still lose money on the other side of the ledger: claims lodged against the wrong plan line item, a price that exceeds the NDIS support item limit, a PRODA access issue nobody's kept on top of, or a plan-managed payment that's gone quiet with no one following it up. Each one is real cash flow sitting in limbo. Building an in-house team to chase all of this is expensive, and it pulls the director's attention straight back into admin instead of growing the organisation.

Why outsource this instead of hiring for it

Three reasons directors move this off their own desk

Costs Less Than an In-House Hire

A dedicated in-house admin or finance role to cover bookkeeping, claims and PRODA is a full salary, on-costs and management overhead. Outsourcing this work costs a fraction of that, at a fixed monthly fee.

Every hour a director spends chasing a plan manager or fixing a rejected claim is an hour not spent on service quality, staffing or growth. Handing this off means that time goes back to running and growing the organisation, where it's worth the most.
