Where SCHADS Payroll Goes Wrong for Regional Providers

The same errors recur across the sector, and the geography of both Ballarat and Bendigo, servicing clients spread across each city and its surrounding towns, makes some of them more consequential:

  • Sleepover shifts recorded as standard hours instead of the correct sleepover allowance
  • Broken shift allowances missed on days where a support worker's roster spans multiple towns with genuine unpaid gaps
  • Travel time under-calculated across a wider service radius than a single-suburb metro operation
  • Classification levels never revisited as a support worker takes on more senior duties
  • The Fair Work Commission's Schedule E changes from 1 October 2026 not yet built into the pay run

A practical way to check your own setup without waiting for an audit: pull one week's roster and one week's pay run side by side for a support worker who did a sleepover shift or worked across two client locations. If the pay run shows a flat number of ordinary hours where the roster shows a sleepover block or a genuine gap between clients, that's the exact pattern that turns into a Fair Work Ombudsman enquiry or a back-pay claim months later, not because anyone meant to underpay, but because the software imported the shift as a flat number instead of what it actually was.

Claims and Payroll, Working Together

For providers who also submit NDIS claims themselves rather than through a plan manager, payroll and claims need to move at the same pace. Claims generally need to be lodged within roughly 30 days of the service date, and a Ballarat or Bendigo provider paying support workers fortnightly can end up funding wages before the matching NDIA payment lands if claims fall behind the roster. We cover this properly, along with what a compliant claim invoice actually needs to include, in our NDIS claim invoice guide and our bookkeeping and claims service page. Providers running payroll and claims through one team, rather than juggling them separately, are the ones who catch this drift before it becomes a cash flow problem.
