Common SCHADS Payroll Mistakes in Regional Victoria

The same handful of errors show up again and again, and providers covering the larger, more spread-out service areas typical around Bendigo tend to hit them harder:

  • Sleepover shifts processed as ordinary hours instead of the correct sleepover allowance
  • Broken shift allowances missed where a support worker's day includes a genuine unpaid gap between client visits
  • Travel time between clients under-calculated across a wide rural or semi-rural service radius
  • Classification set once at hiring and never reviewed as a support worker's actual duties change
  • Award rate updates, including the Fair Work Commission's Schedule E changes from 1 October 2026, not reflected in payroll from the correct effective date
