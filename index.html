What getting it wrong actually costs

A misclassified pay run doesn't look like a crisis. It becomes one.

Award interpretation, sleepover and broken shift allowances, penalty rates, casual loading, they're all quiet places for a generalist payroll process to go wrong. Nothing looks broken in any single pay run. Then a Fair Work audit, a back-pay claim, or a superannuation guarantee charge arrives, and what was a small classification error becomes a five-figure liability and a staff trust problem you can't undo overnight.

Why us specifically

Three things most payroll providers don't do

01

We Run Our Own CPD on SCHADS and Award Changes

The SCHADS Award and the awards covering medical and allied health staff get reviewed and updated regularly, classification thresholds shift, allowance rates move, new determinations land. We run structured internal CPD sessions specifically on SCHADS and Award payroll, so a rate change shows up in your pay runs the pay cycle it takes effect, not months later when someone happens to notice.

02

Your Timesheet Software Actually Talks to Xero

Most NDIS, medical and allied health teams already roster and log time in something like Shiftcare, Ready Roster or Brevity, not in Xero directly. The failure point is almost always in between: shift types that don't map to the right Xero pay items, sleepover or broken-shift entries that get flattened into ordinary hours on import, casual loading applied inconsistently. We build and maintain the actual mapping between your rostering software and Xero's pay items, so what was rostered is what gets paid, every time.

03

Award Classification Reviewed, Not Set and Forgotten

A classification that was correct on someone's first day is rarely still correct a year later once their role has grown. We review classifications on a schedule, not only when something goes wrong, catching the quiet drift before it becomes a wage claim.

04

Full-Service Payroll, Not Just Processing

Award interpretation and classification, superannuation guarantee including one-off Payday Super lodgements outside the regular cycle, Single Touch Payroll filed correctly every pay run plus the annual STP finalisation declaration, state payroll tax threshold monitoring and calculations as your team grows, leave accrual, and the reconciliation between your roster and your pay run, handled end to end. You get one team accountable for the whole thing, not a processor who runs whatever numbers they're handed.
